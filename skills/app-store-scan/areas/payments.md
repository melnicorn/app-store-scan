# payments: in-app purchase, subscriptions, other business models

Guidelines 3.1, 3.2, 4.9, 2.1(b), 5.6. Applies when the app sells or unlocks anything, or links to somewhere that does. If the `maybe` signals turn out to be unrelated wording (for example "unlock" a screen, "credits" in an about box), write a single `PAY-00` check with status `na` explaining that the app has no purchases, and stop. Each check: **ID** `[platform]` (guideline · usual severity when it fails).

First classify what is sold: digital content or features consumed in the app (must use in-app purchase); physical goods or services consumed outside the app (must not use in-app purchase; Apple Pay or card entry); real-time one-to-one services between two people (may use other methods); or access to something bought elsewhere (reader, multiplatform, enterprise exceptions).

**PAY-01** `[all]` (3.1.1 · blocker) Digital goods and features use in-app purchase. Subscriptions, premium features, full-version unlocks, in-game currency, levels and content consumed in the app must go through StoreKit. Fail: Stripe, PayPal, card forms or web checkouts used inside the app for digital goods; license keys, promo-code unlocks outside Apple's offer codes, QR codes, crypto or wallets used to unlock features. `[mac]` Mac App Store apps may enable plug-ins by other means, but may not use license keys for the app itself.

**PAY-02** `[all]` (3.1.1(a), 3.1.3 · blocker) External purchase links and calls to action follow storefront rules. On the United States storefront, buttons, links and calls to action to outside purchasing are allowed without an entitlement. Elsewhere they are prohibited unless the app holds the relevant StoreKit External Purchase Link entitlement (region-specific) or, for reader apps, the External Link Account entitlement. Follow `external_purchase_link` and any "subscribe on our website", "cheaper at", pricing-page or account-upgrade links. If such links exist, check they are limited by storefront (`Storefront.current`) or backed by an entitlement in the entitlements file; otherwise fail for distribution outside the US.

**PAY-03** `[all]` (3.1.1 · high) Restore purchases. Non-consumables and subscriptions need a visible restore control (`AppStore.sync()`, `restoreCompletedTransactions`, or the SDK equivalent) reachable from the paywall or settings.

**PAY-04** `[all]` (3.1.2(c), Schedule 2 · blocker) Subscription terms are disclosed at the point of purchase. The paywall must show: subscription name, length of each period, price (and price per unit if relevant), what the subscriber gets, and working links to the privacy policy and terms of use (EULA). The price actually billed must be the most prominent price; trial terms must say what is charged after and when. Fail when any are missing. `SubscriptionStoreView` supplies most of this if policy and terms links are set.

**PAY-05** `[all]` (3.1.2(a),(b) · high) Subscriptions are legitimate: ongoing value, period of at least seven days, work across the user's devices, no extra tasks required to get what was paid for (posting, inviting, check-ins), clean upgrade/downgrade within one subscription group so users cannot buy two tiers of the same thing, and existing paid customers keep what they bought when a subscription model is introduced.

**PAY-06** `[all]` (3.1.1 · high) Consumables and chance. Purchased credits or currency do not expire. Loot boxes or any randomized paid item disclose odds before purchase. Non-subscription free trials use a price-tier-0 non-consumable named like "14-day Trial" and state duration, what stops working and the cost afterwards.

**PAY-07** `[all]` (3.1.3(e), 4.9 · high) Physical goods and outside services use non-IAP methods and disclose properly. Using in-app purchase for physical goods is itself a violation. Apple Pay flows show all material purchase information before the sale; recurring Apple Pay payments disclose term, what is provided, charges and how to cancel; Apple Pay branding follows the marketing guidelines.

**PAY-08** `[all]` (2.1(b) · high, manual) In-app purchases are reviewable. Every configured product is submitted with the build, visible and functional for the reviewer, with a review screenshot and notes; products in code match product identifiers in App Store Connect or the `.storekit` file. On-device receipt validation supports the SHA-256 certificate, or use StoreKit 2 `Transaction`/`AppTransaction`. Check for a `.storekit` configuration and that purchase failure and pending (Ask to Buy) states are handled.

**PAY-09** `[all]` (3.1.3(a)-(d),(f),(g) · high) Claimed exceptions really apply. Reader apps (previously purchased magazines, newspapers, books, audio, music, video): no in-app purchasing or calls to action outside the US without the entitlement. Multiplatform services: items bought elsewhere may be used if they are also available as in-app purchases. Enterprise: sold only to organizations, not consumers or families. Person-to-person: real-time and one-to-one only; one-to-many must use IAP. Free companion apps to paid web tools (VoIP, cloud storage, email, hosting): no purchasing or purchase prompts in the app. Ad-management apps are exempt, but "boosts" shown in the same app are not.

**PAY-10** `[all]` (3.1.4, 4.10, 3.1.1 · medium) Unlock rules. Features tied to specific hardware may unlock without IAP; optional physical products may unlock features if an IAP path also exists; users cannot be made to buy unrelated products or do marketing tasks. Nothing charges for built-in capabilities (camera, push, iCloud). Tips to the developer or to creators for digital content use IAP; gifts between individuals with 100% going to the recipient and no digital good attached may skip it.

**PAY-11** `[all]` (3, 5.6 · blocker) No deceptive or predatory purchase design. Fail: paywall with a hidden or delayed close button, a trial toggle that hides the real price, price shown per week in large type while billing yearly, countdowns or claims that are fake, purchase prompts that look like system alerts, irrationally high prices for trivial features, pressure on children. Review the paywall view code and copy.

**PAY-12** `[all]` (3.1.5, 3.1.1 NFT · high) Crypto and NFTs. See `regulated` REG-05 for wallets, exchanges and mining. Here: owning an NFT may not unlock app features; NFT minting/listing sold in-app uses IAP; no links to external purchase outside the US.
